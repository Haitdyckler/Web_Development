Hydration Benefits

Physical Performance

Hydration with electrolytes, particularly sodium and potassium, has been found to aid performance during prolonged exercise. Staying hydrated is key for strength, power, and high-intensity endurance.

Learn more

Cognitive Function

Adequate hydration with magnesium is linked to maintenance of normal brain function. Studies show increased brain magnesium may improve cognitive functions such as memory and attention.

Learn more

Muscular Recovery

Electrolytes, especially magnesium, play a significant role in post-exercise recovery. Studies show magnesium may significantly reduce muscle soreness and improve perceived recovery.
